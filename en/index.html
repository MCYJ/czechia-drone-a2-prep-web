Before you begin

Is this an official CAA app?

No. RushLabs provides an independent study aid. It does not register you for an exam, issue a certificate or guarantee a pass.

What does Pro mean?

It is a separate paid app with included content and no additional subscription. Purchases in an older app do not automatically entitle you to Pro.

What language is the study content?

The shown interface and study questions are in Czech. This website also provides English and Korean guidance.

Can I download the Pro app now?

Not yet. Its release is in preparation. A download link will appear only after public availability has been verified.
